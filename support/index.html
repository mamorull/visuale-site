Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Write to support@visualeapp.com — we usually reply within a few days.

Is Visuale free?
Yes. Visuale is in early access: all features are free right now. We will announce any change in advance.
Do I need an account?
No. Your decks and progress sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.
Why don't my cards sync?
Check that you are signed in to iCloud on both devices and that iCloud is enabled for Visuale in Settings → your name → iCloud.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app and, if needed, its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Visuale.
